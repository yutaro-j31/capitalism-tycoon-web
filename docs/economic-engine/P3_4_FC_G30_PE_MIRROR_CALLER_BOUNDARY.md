## 1. Legal source, projection and valuation

- The active `g.peDeals[]` entry is the PE deal source. [`syncPESubsidiary(deal)`](https://github.com/yutaro-j31/capitalism-tycoon-web/blob/1412f0a933a8c21309986e776921155ad6fccf82/js/expansion.js#L681) accepts only `ownerAccount==='company'` and `status==='active'`. It looks up `pe-subsidiary-${deal.id}`, creates it if absent, then updates its ownership, valuation, cost basis and `deal.subsidiaryID`. **It does not remove an already-duplicated same-ID row**, nor does the helper itself call `save()` or `runTransaction()`; the installed enclosing writer supplies the transaction boundary.
- [`companyValue()`](https://github.com/yutaro-j31/capitalism-tycoon-web/blob/1412f0a933a8c21309986e776921155ad6fccf82/js/engine.js#L981) adds `sum(sub.valuation * sub.ownership)` for subsidiaries, but does **not** separately add `g.peDeals[]`. A valid single mirror represents one company-owned PE stake; a second mirror would inflate company value. [`personalNetWorth()`](https://github.com/yutaro-j31/capitalism-tycoon-web/blob/1412f0a933a8c21309986e776921155ad6fccf82/js/expansion.js#L895) adds active non-company PE deal valuations separately.
- [`syncPEDealFromSubsidiary(sub)`](https://github.com/yutaro-j31/capitalism-tycoon-web/blob/1412f0a933a8c21309986e776921155ad6fccf82/js/expansion.js#L682) is a reverse update: only a PE-managed subsidiary linked to an active company-owned deal can update the deal's ownership ratio, current valuation and cost basis. It is not an independent authority or settlement receipt.
