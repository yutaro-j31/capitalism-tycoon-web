For a replacement row, it requires two distinct predecessor IDs, unique replacement ID, explicit lifecycle status, retained completed source evidence with the same IDs/week, and no retained live predecessor. Direct executeGroupRestructuringMerge receipts and completed/completionApplied staged integration projects are supported. Missing/truncated receipts, duplicate/conflicting proof, reused predecessors, cyclic ancestry and temporal conflicts remain unresolved.

Output is lifecycle-only: replacement/predecessor IDs are source IDs, not invented legal issuer identities. Inherited startupID/peDealID/internalVentureID never grant an alias. `parentGenerationStatus` remains unproven. Economic/voting fractions, alias replacement and source snapshot token stay null. complete/wealthComplete/commandEligible stay false. Receipt matching never repairs old saves or establishes a complete past cap table. Sale/liquidation statuses are observed on retained rows; absent rows are not assumed disposed. The bounded reader does not replay transactions or enumerate missing history.

All output is detached/frozen. Only own data descriptors are read; getters, exotic prototypes and malformed selected collections fail closed. Source arrays are not sorted/frozen or passed to existing ensure/preview/writer helpers. No engine, RNG, cash, ledger, save or IDB call occurs inside the reader.
