## Family and source-of-truth boundary

The first candidate is the **current player-company single common class**, with the Phase 1 player-company/founder/class identities. The source facts are `sharesOut`, `treasuryBuybackShares`, `founderShares` and the designated `personalStocks[state.ticker].qty` lot. Issued minus treasury is outstanding; original founder plus designated personal lot is one founder beneficial position; the remainder is unallocated class quantity. Source lots are not additional beneficial positions. Retain source paths and legal-account attribution.

The P3-2 result only certifies those identified quantities. Its residual is not an identified shareholder, beneficial fraction, voting alliance, treasury holding or evidence that legacy external ratios reconcile. Do not sum `competitors[].ownedPlayerShares`, `competitorOwnedRatio`, `externalShareholderRatio`, accepted investor/fund history, `familyTrustShares`, company-owned market aliases or a second personal asset row into that position. Such records require explicit issuer/class/holder evidence and deduplication in their own adapter contract. P2 legacy ratio/receipt scopes remain unchanged.

Adoption requires exact, finite, nonnegative in-envelope quantities and ECO-010/011 source reconciliation. Executable quantity writers must additionally have representable prospective quantities and their command-specific domain; the existing P2 buyback requires integer issued/original-founder/treasury buckets because it floors them. Readable fractional legacy evidence is not permission to round it into an executable position. Missing/contradictory class, holder, beneficial or joint-control evidence stays unsupported. No EPSILON right grants or repairs.

Identity follows the current company slot across renaming and private/public transitions, not matching display names. `foundNewCompanyAfterBuyout` is an explicit replacement of that slot: it increments `currentCompanySerial`, resets company roots and preserves personal assets/market history. Historical issuer interests must not silently become the new company's holdings or voting rights. Ticker aliases alone do not prove cross-generation identity. Later executable adoption must reject ambiguous bindings; preserve the saved assets without guessing or assigning RNG-based identities.

Other external listed equities, startup IPO aliases, listed subsidiaries, `maSubsidiaries`, PE vehicles and VC interests retain existing settlement authority and remain outside this first writer cutover. A subsidiary `publicCompany` flag/percentage/market alias alone is insufficient for ordinary-class/voting/treasury evidence. P3-5/6/7/8 must explicitly map the relevant family before its ownership capability becomes executable. This does not withdraw existing gameplay commands or advance Phase 9/10/13/14 settlement.

## P3-4 writer-adoption requirements

P3-3 records the complete boundary; P3-4 implements only the explicitly cut-over family/facts. Independently migrating command families require separate PRs, but an enabled issuer capability must never accept a partially covered writer set.

1. There must be one authority for each cut-over issued/treasury/holding fact. Specify the API, backing representation, adoption marker/schema if any, and compatibility fields in the P3-4 implementation contract. Legacy fields may be canonical backing storage or derived mirrors; a persisted registry and writable legacy copy must not independently settle the same fact. P3-3 introduces neither a second root nor a migration.
2. Every inventory path that can change or replace the enabled facts must reach that same authority. Until its entire family coverage is proven, keep new executable family capabilities disabled. Do not quietly leave a direct base/helper/shadow/weekly path writable behind a registry that claims completeness. Read-only partial diagnostics remain labeled with their P3-2 scope.
3. Validate pre-state, issuer/generation/source binding and all prospective quantities before economic writes. Commit quantities, existing cash/accounting/mirrors/status/histories and any adopted representation inside one actual outer transaction/shadow boundary. On false/throw/storage failure restore the applicable live state and durable bytes; do not claim engine `runTransaction` covers errors outside its actual work boundary. Preserve existing load/recovery and notification behavior unless a specific failure contract is reviewed.
4. Maintain existing cash/fees/quotes/price impact/ledger effects exactly once. Registry adoption itself is a representation change with no cash, profit, fabricated consideration or historical receipt. P2 dividends and buybacks keep their existing recognition/source IDs/keys; no Phase 9 settlement into Economic Core. Founder secondary sales and personal trades never become company receipts.
5. Distinguish a repeated new trade/buyback from a replay of the same offer/IPO/deal/fund operation. Preserve existing lifecycle guards and finance keys, and prove quantity/status/receipt/cash cannot apply twice for an existing operation. A finance-only duplicate check cannot justify an already-applied second quantity mutation. No new random IDs/draws or clock-dependent eligibility.
6. P3-9 eligibility and P3-10 command effects remain separate. Quantity reconciliation/adoption never grants information/proposal/resolution/MBO/TOB/squeeze-out/management commands automatically; especially >50% or >=2/3 is insufficient by itself. Unknown external/joint/beneficial evidence cannot be made executable by assigning the residual to an invented holder.

## Old-save and replacement boundary

Keep `SAVE_KEY=capitalism_tycoon_web_v1` and `saveVersion=9`. P3-3 writes no adoption marker/root and changes no load normalization. Missing optional old fields remain handled by existing normalization/P3-1 projection; new ownership code must not silently coerce present malformed evidence, repair conservation, prune holdings or rebuild historical ledger events.

For a later registry representation, the implementation contract must cover both absent and present metadata: valid unambiguous old source facts can be adopted exactly once without an economic posting; inconsistent/ambiguous/out-of-domain states preserve their saved quantities and remain unsupported for new ownership capabilities. Present registry metadata that disagrees with backing facts cannot overwrite either side by guesswork. Repeated load/import/adoption must not increase quantities, recognize income or issue shares. Format/version/schema changes require a defined compatibility contract before implementation, not an ad hoc migration during this slice.

Read/import/export, IndexedDB hydration/recovery, every compaction level, transaction restore and shadow clone/replace must retain nonzero holdings and adoption evidence needed to interpret them. Any new authoritative metadata must have explicit compaction retention before activation; otherwise leave it non-persisted/non-authoritative. No expensive/full-registry rebuild on UI renders or pan; pure reads consume no simulation RNG. Physical iPhone validation is distinct from CI WebKit.
