## 3. Scenario model and oracles

Use these logical labels in future test reports; they are **test vocabulary, not persisted IDs or proposed save fields**:

- G1/C1: first company in one new game; `currentCompanySerial=1`.
- SOLD/C1: same company after a successful sale; personal assets/history remain, company ownership binding is no longer active.
- G2/C2: successful refounding in that same game; serial increments, week may be unchanged, root identity remains the installed root. Repeat to G3 to detect hard-coded “not 1” handling.
- P: founder legal personal account. Company accounts C1 and C2 are different legal ownership scopes, even if the UI slot, display name or ticker is reused.

For each future executable case, capture the complete live root, relevant engine transaction state, ownership source buckets, company/personal cash/debt/finance/holdings, startup funded evidence and PE ownership, RNG, localStorage mirror bytes, IDB synchronous cache, durable predecessor bytes, pending put count, sequence, notices and source tokens **before** the command. After failure compare full live state and the storage checkpoint's correct predecessor after flush. After success compare the intended economic state to production reload and a fresh durable-only hydrated browser context, excluding only documented save metadata/normalization (never silently dropping money or ownership fields).

Existing oracle inputs: `tests/helpers/transaction-fault-case.js:40`–`:55` (rollback, pending predecessor, fresh hydration, retry/monotonic sequence); `tests/found-new-company-save-atomicity-test.js:54`–`:91` (installed refounding, root identity and notice timing); `tests/refounding-ledger-test.js:19`–`:32` (contribution, reload and bounded deterministic weeks). SH uses its existing checkpoint; its notice limitations are documented separately in `tests/ma-equity-raise-shadow-atomicity-test.js:24`–`:40`. Do not introduce a replacement rollback protocol.
