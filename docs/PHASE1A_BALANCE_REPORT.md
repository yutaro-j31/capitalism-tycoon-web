| Scenario | Legacy sales | New sales | Gap | Cause | Adjustment | Profit/unit effect |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| New standard store | ~440k | ~435k | -1% | Competitor and outside option offset calibrated demand | Market base multiplier 1.92 | Units become explicit |
| One store | ~440k | ~435k | -1% | Normal price/quality | Kept within ±10% | Margin visible |
| Multiple stores | Store-sum legacy | Store-sum market | expected near ±10% per normal store | Each store has own local market | Per-store capacity and share | Store totals aggregate |
| Price increase | Higher revenue formerly possible via unchanged demand | Demand decreases, sales may rise/fall | Intentional | Elasticity and outside option | Competitor prices use master baseline | Premium allowed by quality/brand |
| Price decrease | Demand did not react directly | Units increase, margin rate falls | Intentional | Price-sensitive segment reacts | Demand is capped | Below-cost can hurt profit |
| Advertising/brand | Demand multiplier only | Trend/brand segment share rises | Intentional | Brand utility | Brand trust included | Premium tolerance rises |
| Quality improvement | Demand multiplier only | Quality segment share rises, variable cost rises | Intentional | Quality utility and quality cost | Quality cost factor added | Profit can improve at right price |
| Low economy | Demand lowered | Market size lowered | Compatible | Economy in market size | Clamped | Finite output |
| High economy | Demand raised | Market size raised | Compatible | Economy in market size | Clamped | Finite output |
| Capacity shortage | No hard cap | Units stop at capacity, lost demand recorded | Intentional | Capacity constraint | Safe zero capacity handling | Opportunity loss shown |

Approved exceptions: extreme price, quality or capacity settings may deviate beyond ±10% because they are now strategy-sensitive.
